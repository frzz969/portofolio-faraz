DEXTRA

Governed Enterprise AI Workforce & Operations Platform

Governance path / select a step
Governance steps, use arrow keys to select a step

01 / RequestA request enters the platform: a task, a question, or an operational action from a defined user.

02 / AnalyzeSpecialized AI workers read the context and supporting data. Four specialists cover Finance, HR, Inventory, and Operations. AI reads and analyzes, nothing is executed here.

03 / RecommendThe workers produce a proposed plan. The recommendation ends at advice: no data is written and no outside system is touched.

04 / ApproveA human reviews the plan. The Policy Validator, a deterministic governance engine (not an AI agent), checks the plan against policy before approval.

05 / ExecuteOnly after an approved plan and an explicit human mandate may an action change data or the outside world.

06 / AuditEvery action leaves an audit record: what was requested, what was approved, what ran, and who mandated it.

AI acts up to recommendation. Writing to data or the outside world needs an approved plan, a human mandate, and an audit record.

AI Detector Mahasiswa

Indonesian Academic Writing Analyzer

A live demo for analyzing Indonesian academic writing. Paste or upload txt, md, pdf, and docx files. It checks 10 language patterns, offers an optional local model around 130MB, and shows a score with highlighted text plus a PDF report, in Indonesian and English. The FAQ is explicit: no detector is 100% accurate, this is bukan pengganti Turnitin, and text stays private in the browser.

Motivation
Explainable analysis for Indonesian academic writing, not automated penalties.
Status
Live demo (prototype). It reads pasted or uploaded txt, md, pdf and docx files, checks 10 language patterns, and shows a score with highlighted text plus a PDF report. The score is an uncertain indicator, not proof of AI use or an academic-integrity verdict. No detector is 100% accurate, and this is not a replacement for Turnitin.
